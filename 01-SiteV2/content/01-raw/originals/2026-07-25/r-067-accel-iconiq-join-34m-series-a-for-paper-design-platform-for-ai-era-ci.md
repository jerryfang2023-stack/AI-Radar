# Accel, Iconiq Join $34M Series A for Paper, Design Platform for AI Era | citybiz

## clean_text

Skip to content
A neon ad for Paper in San Francisco.
Paper , which calls itself a design platform for the agentic era, has announced the closing of a $34 million Series A funding round led by Accel and Iconiq . Other participating investors included Designer Fund, Michael Grinich of WorkOS, Anton Osika of Lovable , and engineers and designers from Anthropic and OpenAI .
The San Francisco startup said it will use the new funding to accelerate product development, expand its team, and deepen its investment in AI-native workflows for designers.
“We want to help designers and creatives do deep, meaningful work, at a pace that supports their businesses in the agentic era. Helping design teams keep up with agentic workflows is our north star at Paper,” said Paper founder and CEO Stephen Haney , a game developer who previously headed engineering at the developer platform WorkOS.
“Designers are being asked to move faster than ever, yet they’re still working with tools built for a different time. We believe there is a massive opportunity for a new generation of tools that help designers do their best work while working seamlessly with…coding agents like Claude Code and Codex.”
One of the biggest changes brought about by AI, unsurprisingly, is speed. It also has made routine tasks a lot easier and faster. Vercel’s v0 platform or Canva allows designers to build simple visual concepts and working layouts with simple text instructions.
In this emerging environment, Paper began with the launch of Paper Desktop earlier this year. Paper Desktop turns the design canvas into a shared workspace for humans and AI agents — enabling design, code, and data in real time. Today, Paper renders with HTML and CSS, enabling designs to connect directly with production code, with AI agents playing a growing role.
“Paper is defining a new category with a fundamentally new approach to design software,” said Accel partner Dan Levine . “Most design tools were built for a world where humans did all the work. Paper is built for a world where humans and AI create together.”
Paper says it has grown annualized recurring revenue (ARR) 25x since launch, appearing on Ramp’s Fastest Growing Companies list for four consecutive months. Its customers include Ramp, Lovable, Vercel, PostHog, Quartr and Y Combinator.
