E4 wins the tender for Luxembourg’s AI Factory
22 July 2026
The EuroHPC Joint Undertaking has signed a supply contract with E4 Computer Engineering, the company selected as the contractor following a call for tenders launched in February 2026, for the procurement, delivery, installation and maintenance of MeluXina-AI, the new AI-optimised supercomputer intended for the Luxembourg AI Factory.
The EuroHPC Joint Undertaking has signed a supply contract with E4 MeluXina-AI, which forms part of the Luxembourg AI Factory initiative and will be hosted and managed by LuxProvide . The new system will strengthen Europe’s capabilities in artificial intelligence, machine learning and data science, providing the computing power r equired for the training, testing and pre-production deployment of advanced AI models, and supporting research, innovation and industrial development in strategic sectors such as finance, space, cybersecurity and the green economy . The system will be complemented by a rich and scalable software ecosystem, designed to meet the needs of start-ups, companies of all sizes, researchers and industry. The project will be co-funded with a total budget of 80 million euros, 50 per cent of which will be covered by the EuroHPC JU through the Digital Europe Programme (DEP). puter Engineering, a company selected as the contractor following a call for tenders launched in February 2026, for the procurement, delivery, installation and maintenance of MeluXina-AI, the new AI-optimised supercomputer intended for the Luxembourg AI Factory.
A next-generation infrastructure
Integrated by E4 Computer Engineering, The MeluXina-AI supercomputer will be built on Dell Technologies servers with liquid-cooled NVIDIA GB200 NVL4
systems based on the Blackwell architecture across 252 nodes for a total of 1,008 GPUs and a multi-protocol file system for both AI and HPC tiers. The supercomputer will be deployed on a dual-site infrastructure, Bissen and Bettembourg, delivering an estimated AI peak performance of over 20 Exaflops. Dell Technologies will participate in the delivery of the contract as a subcontractor.
The software stack developed for MeluXina-AI will enable the provision of a GPU-as-a-Service offering to multiple tenants, with native container isolation and dynamic workload scheduling. System governance (Smart Governance) will be provided by E4’s software, whilst the AI application ecosystem will be delivered via a cloud-native stack based on the Urania MLOps platform, developed by E4.
The infrastructure will support secure multi-tenant operations through isolated cloud-native environments, protecting sensitive data and intellectual property . Through integrated compute and data bridges, the platform will also ensure connectivity with external data platforms, cloud providers and the other EuroHPC AI Factories, contributing to a seamless end-to-end computing environment for AI workloads across Europe. Sovereign cloud capabilities will also enable the deployment of AI applications in secure and sovereign environments, ensuring control, security and regulatory compliance.
E4 Computer Engineering was selected following a call for tenders launched in February 2026. Designed primarily to support start-ups and SMEs in their research and innovation activities, MeluXina-AI will also be accessible to the wider research community. Installation work is scheduled to begin in the fall of 2026 at the two sites in Bissen and Bettembourg, Luxembourg.
“Another success recognises E4’s commitment to European innovation! Following on from IT4LIA, the Italian AI Factory, we are delighted to announce that we will also be building Meluxina AI, the supercomputer for Luxembourg’s AI Factory. These are two major milestones for E4 Computer Engineering, made possible by our team’s unique expertise, which establishes us as a European leader in HPC and Artificial Intelligence,” said our CEO, Cosimo Damiano Gianfreda.
The Luxembourg AI Factory and the European AI Factory ecosystem
Launched in April 2025, the Luxembourg AI Factory s erves as a central hub for innovation and the adoption of AI, offering services to start-ups, SMEs and other European users through the existing MeluXina supercomputer. The initiative is coordinated by LuxProvide and is being implemented in collaboration with five consortium members, including Luxinnovation, the Luxembourg National Data Service, the University of Luxembourg and the Luxembourg Institute of Science and Technology. With the signing of this contract, the initiative enters its next phase: MeluXina-AI will support the development of reliable, high-performance AI solutions, creating new opportunities for innovation in Luxembourg and across Europe.
The Luxembourg AI Factory is part of a wider network of AI Factories across Europe: the EuroHPC JU is coordinating the roll-out of 19 AI Factories offering free, tailored support to SMEs and start-ups, alongside a further 13 AI Factory Antennas.
Following the award of the IT4LIA contract in Italy, E4 is thus strengthening its position amongst Europe’s leading players in the field of AI Factories, bringing its expertise, technology and vision beyond national borders. This achievement confirms E4’s ability to collaborate with the most innovative technology partners to build infrastructure that supports European digital sovereignty.
