AI hardware startup Aina has raised $5.5 Mn in a seed funding round co-led by Redstart Labs and 360 ONE Asset👇

Vorflux , the new startup founded by former Rippling Co-founder & CTO Prasanna Sankar has raised $15 million in seed funding from Y Combinator , Peak XV Partners , Alliance, and Rippling co-founder Parker Conrad , among other investors.
Sankar describes Vorflux as the "autopilot for software engineering," aiming to move beyond today's AI copilots by enabling AI to execute larger portions of the software development lifecycle autonomously.
💡 "The models quietly got good enough to fly the whole route, but the tools never caught up," Sankar said, highlighting the company's vision for the next generation of AI-native software engineering.
The startup is also engaging directly with developers, inviting engineering teams to share their biggest coding bottlenecks in exchange for $200 in free credits.
As AI rapidly transforms software development, startups like Vorflux are betting that the future belongs not just to #AI assistants—but to autonomous engineering platforms.
