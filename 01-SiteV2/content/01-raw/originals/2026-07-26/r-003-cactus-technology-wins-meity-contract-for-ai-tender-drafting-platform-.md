## clean_text

Cactus Technology wins MeitY contract for AI tender drafting platform
Cactus Technology Solutions has secured a contract from the IT Ministry to develop an AI-driven platform for drafting government procurement documents. This initiative aims to enhance efficiency and compliance in the procurement process.
Mumbai-based Cactus Technology Solutions has been awarded a contract by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) to create an artificial intelligence (AI)-powered platform designed to assist government departments in drafting, reviewing, and validating procurement documents.The project, commissioned by the National e-Governance Division (NeGD), aims to streamline the preparation of Requests for Proposals (RFPs), Requests for Quotations (RFQs), and Expressions of Interest (EOIs).Cactus outbid Kyndryl Solutions with a bid of ₹2.27 crore, while Kyndryl's bid was ₹2.86 crore. In the quality-and-cost based selection (QCBS) evaluation, Cactus received a score of 100, surpassing Kyndryl's score of 85.36.Akhilesh Ayer, CEO of Cactus Technology Solutions, expressed pride in winning the mandate, highlighting the potential for increased efficiency and transparency in government procurement, which constitutes nearly 22% of India's GDP.The platform will be deployed across various ministries and departments, providing an AI-assisted tool for drafting and standardizing procurement documents while ensuring human oversight remains central to the process.
