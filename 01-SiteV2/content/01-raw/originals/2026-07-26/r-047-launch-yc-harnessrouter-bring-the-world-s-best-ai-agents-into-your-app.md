HarnessRouter: Bring the World's Best AI Agents Into Your App, With One API
One API that puts Codex, Claude Code, Hermes, and more behind your product, so your users get real output: videos, games, code, documents.
Richard Song
Epsilla
2 days ago
Embed
https://www.epsilla.com/ #ai #saas #databases #infrastructure
Hey everyone 👋 Today we're introducing HarnessRouter.
TL;DR: HarnessRouter lets builders bring Codex, Claude Code, Hermes, and other agents into your products through one API. Each task runs in an isolated sandbox with step-by-step tracing and configurable cost caps. It's designed to scale from consumer apps to large-scale model evaluations, orchestrating thousands of isolated sandbox runs in parallel. Start with our AGENTS.md , drop it into your coding agent, describe want you want to build, add an API key, and ship without rebuilding agent runtime infrastructure.
Try it now: harnessrouter.ai
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vZXvgYS7_lg
The Problem
There's a gap between what AI agents can do and what your product can offer.
Agents like Codex and Claude Code can genuinely finish work now, but they're built for developers, not for your users. Bringing that capability to your users inside your own product means building the infrastructure yourself: isolated execution environments, agent runtime, tool orchestration, file handling, streaming, retries, permissions, cost controls. Every team building an AI feature rebuilds this same stack from scratch, and it takes months. After launch, the upgrades and maintenance never stop.
As a result, most teams ship a chat interface instead, when what users actually need is finished output.
The Solution
HarnessRouter runs the entire agent stack as a service. Your product sends a task through one API; We run it on the agent you choose and return the result in structured, renderable form: reviewable diffs, generated files, images, video, confirmed tool actions.
Teams are already running production workloads on it:
🏥 A top medical research institution is connecting its operational data into a unified agent layer, an "academic brain" for the organization.
✅ A leading healthcare compliance company encodes domain expertise into agent skills, saving their clients hundreds of hours of manual work per audit.
🎬 An AI video marketing startup drives its content generation pipeline through HarnessRouter, producing viral-grade video at human quality.
Integration takes three steps: drop our AGENTS.md into your coding agent, describe the feature you want and add your API key. Switching agents is a one-line configuration change, and the same setup scales from a single task to thousands of concurrent runs.
Who We Are
Kuanze Ma, Co-founder
Kuanze is a four-time founder focused on human–AI systems, with experience in systems design, product management, algorithms, recruiting, marketing, and engineering. He holds a BSc in Mathematics, a diploma in Software Engineering, and an MSL from Northwestern University.
Richard Song, Co-founder
Richard has over a decade of experience building large-scale systems, including vertical AI agents powered by private data and domain knowledge. Previously, he was a Senior Director at TigerGraph and a Senior Software Engineer at Meta. He holds an MS in Computer Science from Cornell University and a BS in Computer Science.
Our Ask
If you're building an AI feature , start with the free 7-day trial. We'll help you integrate.
If you need to run thousands of agents in parallel , for model evaluations, batch generation, or large-scale pipelines, that's what the sandbox orchestration layer was built for. Talk to us.
If you've built your own agent infrastructure , we'd value the comparison. Tell us what we're missing.
Intros welcome to any team that wants to ship agent-powered features without building the backend.
Reach us at contact@harnessrouter.ai
