# TKMS and Cohere sign a contract to deploy an AI data integration platform for the TKMS Group - TKMS Group

TKMS and Cohere sign a contract to deploy an AI data integration platform for the TKMS Group
29.06.26
The contract covers the deployment of Cohere’s North platform and related project services for TKMS.
The project supports the introduction of a group-wide AI data and integration platform and reinforces TKMS’s commitment to secure, responsible AI and Canadian industrial participation.
Kiel/Toronto, June 29, 2026 – TKMS and Cohere, the world’s leading sovereign AI company, have signed a contract for IT services. The contract marks the next phase of the companies’ collaboration and covers the deployment of Cohere’s North platform and related project services to support the introduction of a group-wide AI data and integration platform at TKMS.
“Canada’s next-generation submarine project presents an opportunity to bring the most modern and reliable technologies to the Royal Canadian Navy. Now, another CPSP MoU has been converted into a full-fledged contract. Cohere’s expertise in trustworthy AI models aligns perfectly with our vision and together, we aim to enhance the user experience and operational efficiency of future crews while maintaining the highest security and safety standards,” said Thomas Keupp, Chief Sales Officer, TKMS.
The eight-figure contract is the first assignment under the framework agreement between TKMS and Cohere, which serves as the basis for collaboration on various research and product projects. Under the agreement, Cohere will provide its secure North platform and cutting-edge AI models, support TKMS in establishing a group-wide AI-powered data platform for internal business purposes, and develop selected software connections to integrate relevant enterprise systems and data environments.
Cohere’s North platform enables enterprises that prioritize data security to deploy AI agents and automations at scale within their own infrastructure. For TKMS, the deployment is intended to improve access to organizational knowledge, strengthen digital workflows, save time and support the secure use of enterprise data in complex engineering and defence environments.
"Cohere is helping reduce cognitive load for sailors while delivering the precision, security, and streamlined operations required in these high-stakes environments," said Dave Ferris, Vice President, Global Public Sector at Cohere. "Sovereign AI capabilities are particularly critical in submarine operations, where autonomous decision-making and secure data processing must remain under complete operational control. Our technology ensures that critical AI functions can operate independently even in disconnected environments, maintaining mission continuity while preserving national security protocols."
The project builds on the Teaming Agreement announced by TKMS and Cohere in January 2026 to explore AI-enabled capabilities for the Canadian Patrol Submarine Project (CPSP). With this contract, the companies are moving from strategic cooperation into implementation, with a focus on secure enterprise AI, knowledge management, data integration, and workflow support.
The contract further underscores TKMS’s long-term commitment to working with Canadian industry partners and applying advanced technologies in a secure, responsible, and mission-focused way. The cooperation is also intended to support TKMS’s wider Canadian industrial strategy, including potential alignment with Canada’s Industrial and Technological Benefits policy in connection with the Canadian Patrol Submarine Project.
