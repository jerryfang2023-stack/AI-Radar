# Sheba Medical Center Partners with OpenAI in First International ChatGPT for Healthcare Deployment

## clean_text

What You Should Know
Israel’s Sheba Medical Center (ranked among Newsweek’s World’s Best Hospitals for 8 consecutive years) has entered an official partnership with OpenAI to execute the first global, hospital-wide rollout of ChatGPT for Healthcare .
Gives physicians, nurses, researchers, and operational staff access to generative AI models pre-calibrated to synthesize peer-reviewed medical literature, clinical guidelines, and internal hospital protocols with full, verifiable citations.
Led by Prof. Eyal Zimlichman and Technology Director Alon Agmon within Sheba’s ARC (Accelerate, Redesign, Collaborate) ecosystem to establish a blueprinted model for fully AI-powered health systems.
Incorporates Sheba’s custom clinical pathways, policy documents, and care protocols directly into the LLM workspace while providing early API access for research.
Built with zero-data-retention and non-training guarantees, ensuring patient data and clinical outputs remain isolated and regulatory-compliant.
OpenAI’s ARC Collaboration Inks First International Hospital Deal with Sheba
The global healthcare ecosystem, enterprise medical informatics, and hospital operational management sectors are entering a new phase of AI adoption. While health systems have experimented with point-solution LLMs for ambient scribing or simple administrative tasks, broad clinical adoption has been limited by concerns over evidence verification, hallucination risks, and data privacy.
Clinicians spend significant time navigating fragmented EHR interfaces, searching through siloed clinical databases, and completing documentation. Without an enterprise-grade reasoning layer capable of synthesizing peer-reviewed literature alongside internal health system protocols, clinical decision support remains slow and disconnected.
To solve workflow fragmentation and build the operational infrastructure for a fully AI-powered hospital, Sheba Medical Center has announced a collaboration with OpenAI . Driven by the ARC (Accelerate, Redesign, Collaborate) innovation arm , the agreement marks OpenAI’s first international deployment of ChatGPT for Healthcare across a complete hospital network.
Evidence Synthesis and ARC Ecosystem Integration
The deployment embeds OpenAI’s specialized models into Sheba’s clinical, research, and operational workflows:
Evidence-Grounded Clinical Search: Synthesizes millions of peer-reviewed journal papers and public health guidelines, attaching direct citations (journal name, authors, and publication dates) to every generated output.
Institutional Knowledge Ingestion: Connects Sheba’s approved care pathways, diagnostic protocols, and administrative policies directly into the workspace via Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC).
Bidirectional R&D Feedback Loop: Sheba receives early API access to OpenAI’s newest foundation models (e.g., GPT-5 class engines) while providing real-world clinical feedback to refine future healthcare AI models.
Enterprise Security Architecture: Operating under zero-data retention policies where patient information is never used to train OpenAI’s base models, meeting enterprise healthcare security and regulatory standards.
“We are not just implementing AI in medicine but building a fully AI-powered hospital, and providing secure access to the world’s most advanced AI is a decisive step in that transformation,” stated Prof. Eyal Zimlichman, Chief Innovation and AI Officer at Sheba Medical Center and Director of ARC. “Physicians, nurses, and researchers at Sheba will now have a powerful clinical reasoning tool at their fingertips, one that surfaces the best available evidence in seconds.”
As Sheba and the ARC AI Center begin rolling out ChatGPT for Healthcare in the coming months, they provide a blueprint for how modern medical centers will transition into fully integrated, AI-powered health systems.
