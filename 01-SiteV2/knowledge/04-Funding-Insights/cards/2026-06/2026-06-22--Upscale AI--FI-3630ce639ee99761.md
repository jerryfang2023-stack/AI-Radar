# Upscale AI｜$190 million｜Series A-1 (A轮扩展)

> [!summary] 公司概况
> 一家纯AI网络基础设施公司，通过开放标准、全栈、交钥匙解决方案实现异构计算，其产品组合专为超低延迟网络构建，可在AI训练、推理和生成式AI部署中实现突破性性能和可扩展性。

## 产品

### AI网络全栈组合（硅、系统、软件）

通过统一的开放标准AI架构，将加速器、内存和存储连接成单一高性能AI引擎，专为消除大规模同步AI工作负载（包括前沿模型的训练和推理）中的网络瓶颈而构建。

- **目标客户**：超大规模云服务商和领先的新云基础设施提供商
- **关键能力**：
  - 全栈组合涵盖硅、系统和软件
  - 统一开放标准AI架构
  - 消除大规模同步AI工作负载的网络瓶颈

### SkyHammer芯片（Scale-up交换机）

提供确定性延迟的定制芯片，支持UALink和ESUN等开放协议，使GPU能像访问本地内存一样读取彼此的内存，避免数据移动速度的意外停顿。

- **目标客户**：AI数据中心
- **关键能力**：
  - 确定性延迟
  - 支持UALink开放协议
  - 支持ESUN（AI优化以太网）

### 基于NVIDIA Spectrum-X的Scale-out交换机

基于NVIDIA Spectrum-X硅构建的交换机产品线，搭配AI优化版SONiC网络操作系统，旨在简化运行。

- **目标客户**：AI数据中心
- **关键能力**：
  - 基于NVIDIA Spectrum-X硅
  - 搭配AI优化版SONiC操作系统

## 资本判断

> [!analysis] 应用层判断
> 资本押注的核心变量是AI基础设施价值从计算层向网络层的迁移。Upscale AI在成立约一年内以$2B估值完成$500M总融资，估值建立在其创始团队深厚的网络芯片背景（来自Innovium、Cavium、Intel、Palo Alto Networks等）、NVIDIA作为战略投资者的背书、以及多家超大规模云服务商和新云提供商正在进行产品评估的早期商业验证之上。然而，公司尚未交付商业化产品，收入验证缺失，估值远超收入基础。

### 已验证信号

- 创始团队拥有多次网络芯片创业和退出的深厚行业背景
- NVIDIA作为战略投资者参与本轮，且Upscale的scale-out交换机基于NVIDIA Spectrum-X硅构建
- 多家超大规模云服务商和新云基础设施提供商正在进行产品评估和部署
- AI数据中心交换机市场预计到2030年超过$1000亿/年（Dell'Oro Group预测）
- 公司从成立到$2B估值仅用约一年时间，反映资本对AI基础设施层的强烈需求

### 证据边界与风险

- 产品仍处于客户评估阶段，尚未实现商业化规模收入，估值远超当前业务基本面
- 面临NVIDIA自有Spectrum-X交换机、Broadcom、Arista和Cisco等既有巨头的激烈竞争，市场进入壁垒高

### [Upscale AI Raises $190 Million as Nvidia Joins the Round](<https://artofthestart.com/upscale-ai-raises-190-million-nvidia/>)

> a $200 million Series A the company closed in January

> a year-old startup building networking chips purpose-made for artificial intelligence data centers

> Broadcom and Arista Networks dominate the merchant switching market.

> Cisco, the company Upscale's investors openly compare it to, remains the incumbent giant of enterprise networking.

> has already grown to around 200 employees

> Nvidia sells its own Spectrum-X switches.

> Nvidia, whose chips Upscale's switches are built to serve, came in as a strategic investor.

> On the scale-out side, Upscale is leaning directly on Nvidia. In March it previewed a switch line built around Nvidia's Spectrum-X silicon, paired with SONiC, an open-source network operating system Microsoft originally created for its own cloud. Upscale has built an AI-tuned version of SONiC meant to make the notoriously fiddly software easier to run.

> Spending on AI data center switches is forecast to top $100 billion a year by 2030, according to Dell'Oro Group

> The startup was founded only in 2025

> The startup was founded only in 2025 by Kar and Rajiv Khemani

> Upscale is betting that an AI-only design built on open standards can win business from buyers wary of being locked into a single vendor.

> Upscale says its scale-up line delivers what it calls deterministic latency, meaning data-movement speeds can be known in advance and unexpected stalls avoided. The capability runs on a custom chip the company calls SkyHammer, which supports open protocols built for this kind of traffic, including UALink, a standard that lets GPUs read one another's memory as if it were local, and ESUN, an AI-tuned flavor of Ethernet.

> Upscale went from founding to a $2 billion valuation in roughly a year

> Upscale's wager is that a clean-sheet design free of legacy baggage, built around open standards rather than a single vendor's proprietary stack, can win share as buyers look to avoid lock-in.
