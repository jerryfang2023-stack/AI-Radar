# V4.5.2 Reviewed Founder Profiles Audit

## Outcome

- Added a first reviewed batch of 30 founder profiles to the V4 entity service.
- Founder profiles are published only when the person role, company identity, same-source quote, source URL, content hash, and quote hash all pass the review contract.
- The public entity index now contains 61 people: 31 existing reviewed people and 30 reviewed funding founders.
- The 30 founder profiles link to their funding research records and original evidence. The remaining 396 funding-person candidates stay deferred and are not published as founder profiles.
- Founder evidence does not create `RELATION-V2.1` edges without an accepted canonical event and Claim lineage. The relationship table remains at 356 records.

## Contract And Prevention

- Introduced `PERSON-REVIEW-V1.1` funding-founder review decisions with an explicit 30-person allowlist.
- The assertion requires the stored reviewed-name set to equal the configured allowlist exactly and rejects duplicate names or stable person IDs.
- Company identity is accepted only when the reviewed company name and full name normalize to the same subject and the founder quote explicitly contains that company subject.
- Stored review provenance is revalidated against the current funding source bundles so source or quote drift fails the gate.
- `assert:funding-founders` is part of the Data Center synchronization path.
