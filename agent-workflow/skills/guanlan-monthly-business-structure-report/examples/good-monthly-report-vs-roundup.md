# Monthly Writing Examples

These are method examples, not historical conclusions or reusable facts.

A useful structural paragraph identifies a supported action, explains the affected buyer or cost, then distinguishes the observed result from an editorial inference. Several independent accepted events can support a trend; one funding announcement cannot prove customer demand.

A weak paragraph lists weekly headlines and concludes that AI is growing quickly. Adding a long citation list does not make it complete.

A useful opportunity card names a buyer with a concrete workflow problem, evaluates existing alternatives, defines a small deliverable and explains who might pay and why. Any unconfirmed supply gap or willingness to pay stays a hypothesis.

Use the six sections and visible-body minimums from the current template. Do not copy sample industry conclusions, past dates, historical titles or verification checklists.
