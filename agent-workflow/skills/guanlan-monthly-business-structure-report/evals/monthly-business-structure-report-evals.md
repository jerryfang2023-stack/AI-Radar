# Monthly Business Structure Report Evals

1. Complete calendar month: accepts only the resolved month's full E/O/C evidence, never an earliest-record slice or previous report prose.
2. Structure: six complete numbered sections, with no data-boundary, contradiction/counterevidence or next-month checklist modules.
3. Substantive length: at least 6,000 reader-visible characters and section-level minimums; long citation IDs and repeated metadata cannot rescue a short report.
4. Factual discipline: accepted E supports events; O/C remain attributed interpretation and demand context. Planned deployments, fundraising and customer revenue remain distinct.
5. Monthly reasoning: value chain, buyers, supply, costs and governance have examples, mechanisms, consequences and limitations. At least three trends are adjudicated; at least one remains under observation or is downgraded.
6. Opportunities: two or three developed cards identify buyers, demand, substitutes, supply gaps, minimum products, commercialization, risks and reasoned scores.
7. Headline: Flash title uses the completed report and stays within its evidence.
8. Acceptance: reject placeholders, unsupported IDs, incomplete sections and any attempt to render before the content gate passes.
