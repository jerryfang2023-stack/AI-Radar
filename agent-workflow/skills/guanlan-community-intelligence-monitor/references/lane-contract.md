# Community Intelligence Lane Contract

The Community Intelligence lane owns:

- local logged-in community collection;
- community frontstage data;
- daily repository snapshots;
- downstream Guanlan Vault projection after accepted data reaches local `main`;
- community data gate reports;
- community automation PR publication.

It must not own:

- V4 Claims, CanonicalEvents, or factual relationships;
- relationship graph or trend-candidate evidence;
- First-Line Viewpoints data;
- verified business facts unless the original source is separately captured and passes the full SourceArtifact → RawDocument → exact-span Claim → CanonicalEvent chain.
